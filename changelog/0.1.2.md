## New

- The budget page has a ⋯ menu in its header: duplicate, export as .md, move to a view, archive or delete the budget without going back to the list.

## Improved

- Deleting a budget from its page asks for confirmation first.
- Archiving or deleting a budget from its page takes you back to where you opened it from, with your filters kept.
- An archived budget's menu offers Unarchive.

## Fixed

- Redoing an archive or delete on an open budget (⇧⌘Z) goes back to where you opened it from, not the unfiltered budget list.
