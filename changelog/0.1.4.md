## New

- Arrow keys move between budgets, expenses, sidebar items and settings rows. Press Enter to open the one you're on.
- Every budget in the list view can now be reached from the keyboard.

## Improved

- On the budgets page, E (archive) and ⌘⌫ (delete) act on the budget you moved to with the arrow keys.
- The keyboard shortcuts list includes the arrow keys.
