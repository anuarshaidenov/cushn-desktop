## New

- Drag across the grid or list to select several budgets, then archive or delete them in one go.
- Hold ⌘ for a moment to reveal shortcut hints across the app.

## Improved

- Selection works the same in grid and list views, with ⌘-click to add or remove a single budget.
- E and ⌘⌫ act on the hovered budget when nothing is selected.

## Fixed

- Shortcut hints no longer flash on a quick ⌘C or ⌘K.
